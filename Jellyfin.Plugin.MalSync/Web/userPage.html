Get started

    Connect your MyAnimeList account

    MAL Sync opens MyAnimeList in a new tab so you can grant access. Your MAL password is never seen by Jellyfin.

    1. Approve access in the new tab
      Log in to MyAnimeList if asked, then choose Allow.
    2. Copy the address you land on
      The page will look broken — that is expected and means it worked. Copy the whole address from your browser's address bar and paste it below.
    Starts with http://localhost/?code=

    Send your progress to MyAnimeList

    Looks at the anime you've watched in Jellyfin and updates the episode count and status on your MyAnimeList list to match.

    A test run reports exactly what would change without touching your MAL list.

    What should be requested

    A rule picks anime off your MyAnimeList list by status and requests it in Jellyseerr under your own account. Without at least one rule nothing is requested.

    How your anime is matched

    Every season in your anime libraries is paired with an entry on MyAnimeList. Where the automatic match is wrong, correct it here — your correction is remembered and used from then on.
